Weltall {version} is a personal fork of [Weltall](https://github.com/Roman215/Weltall), the native port of
*Xenogears* (PlayStation, 1998) rebuilt in Unreal Engine 5. It is the 1.0.3 release with the changes below.
You'll need your own copy of the USA release or a supported English undub. No game data is included.

This fork is not the original project: please report problems with it here, not to the original.

## What's new

- **A Gear's floor shadow stays under its feet.** The round shadow under a Gear on the field no longer
  paints across its boots: it now meets the model at the sole and stops. A 3D model always stands in
  front of the shadow on its floor, so the shadow can no longer cut across its outline.
- **The party and its shadows keep up with the camera.** Turning the free camera, the characters, their
  shadows and the map's effects no longer trail the ground by a fraction of a tick.
- The fork now sits on the original's 1.0.3 release, so the original's own additions come with it:
  compass placement under **Options → Display**, the optional **Field fast-forward** under
  **Options → Modding → Balance**, two-button controller shortcuts under **Options → Controls**, filling
  a 4:3 display, and two fixes (a second controller as player one, and falling back to the disc's assets
  when an asset folder goes away).

The fork's earlier release carried the **see-through scenery** about the party, the **steadier 3D** under
the free camera, and the **message boxes** drawn at a whole number of display pixels; those are here too.

## Playing

1. Download the Windows zip below and unzip it wherever you want.
2. Put both disc images in the `Games` folder. BIN/CUE, raw IMG and CHD made from raw images preserve movie
   audio. Movies are silent with 2048-byte data-only images.
3. Run `Weltall.exe` on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit, DirectX 12).

This is a Development build, the one the fork is tested with: a little larger than a release build, and the
same game. The title screen shows the version, {version}, so you can tell this build from the original's. Settings and
saves are in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Weltall`, the same place as the original's, so the two share them.

See the original's [README](https://github.com/Roman215/Weltall#playing) for controls and everything else.
