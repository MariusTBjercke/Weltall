Weltall {version} is a personal fork of [Weltall](https://github.com/Roman215/Weltall), the native port of
*Xenogears* (PlayStation, 1998) rebuilt in Unreal Engine 5. It is the 1.0.1 release with the changes below.
You'll need your own copy of the USA release or a supported English undub. No game data is included.

This fork is not the original project: please report problems with it here, not to the original.

## What's new

- **See-through scenery.** Trees, rocks, pillars and walls that stand between the camera and your party are
  thinned out in a round bubble around them, so you can see where you walk. Only what actually hides someone
  is thinned out; the ground you stand on and the scenery beside you stay. It fades in and out, and a moment
  behind a ledge (a jump) does not set it off. Cutscenes keep the original framing. It is off by default:
  turn it on under **Options → Display → See-through scenery**, with the bubble Small, Normal or Large.
- **Steadier 3D with the free camera.** Parts of the Gears' models no longer blink in and out as you turn the
  camera, shadows no longer lose half of themselves, and sprites, effects and the save point are no longer
  sorted wrongly when the camera is zoomed or turned.
- **Message boxes**: **Options → Display → Text box size** draws the boxes at whole pixels (or a step
  smaller), and **Text box shading** can make their background solid. Both keep the original look by default.

## Playing

1. Download the Windows zip below and unzip it wherever you want.
2. Put both disc images in the `Games` folder. BIN/CUE, raw IMG and CHD made from raw images preserve movie
   audio. Movies are silent with 2048-byte data-only images.
3. Run `Weltall.exe` on Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit, DirectX 12).

The title screen shows the version, {version}, so you can tell this build from the original's. Settings and
saves are in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Weltall`, the same place as the original's, so the two share them.

See the original's [README](https://github.com/Roman215/Weltall#playing) for controls and everything else.
